Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about adding watermarks to PDF files.

Can I add a text watermark?

Yes. Enter your own text and customize its color, size, transparency, position and rotation.

Can I use my company logo?

Yes. Upload a JPG or PNG image and position it visually on the PDF.

Can I watermark every page?

Yes. Select All Pages to apply the watermark throughout the document.

Can I make the watermark transparent?

Yes. Use the transparency slider to make the watermark lighter or stronger.
