Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Sign PDF tool.

Can I add multiple signatures to one PDF page?

Yes. Upload one signature and use Copy Signature to create as many copies as needed.

Can every signature be moved separately?

Yes. Each signature can be selected, moved, resized and rotated separately.

Can I delete one signature without removing the others?

Yes. Select the signature and click Delete Selected.

Can I sign different pages?

Yes. Change pages with Previous and Next, then add signatures to the required pages.
